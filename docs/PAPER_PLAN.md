---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the publication strategy for the Adaptive Multi-Agent Framework.

Its objectives are

• Plan publications before experiments begin

• Ensure every experiment contributes to one or more papers

• Avoid duplicate work

• Track manuscript progress

• Track journal submissions

• Maintain a publication roadmap throughout the PhD

Every experiment should reference at least one planned publication.

---

# 2. Research Vision

The long-term objective is to develop a complete adaptive AI framework for non-invasive hemoglobin estimation that contributes

• Novel datasets

• Novel segmentation methods

• Novel Hb prediction models

• Novel adaptive decision-making framework

• Open-source research software

• Clinical deployment framework

The work should result in multiple high-quality journal publications rather than one oversized manuscript.
